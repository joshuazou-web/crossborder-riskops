Rules version `1.0.0` - 20 deterministic rules.

These rules run against **synthetic** data generated by a seeded script. Any firing count or precision figure quoted for them describes that synthetic population, not a real payment estate.

**No language model participates in detection.** Every signal in this product is produced by the code documented below, is reproducible from the same input, and names the exact fields it was computed from. That last property is what makes "the copilot made an ungrounded claim" a number rather than an opinion: the copilot may only cite from these fields.

## Severity weights

Severities combine with a noisy-OR, not a sum. Summing would let four low-severity signals outrank one critical signal, which is exactly backwards.

## Rules

### `R101_DUPLICATE_IDEMPOTENCY`

**Duplicate payment under one idempotency key** · family `duplication` · severity `high` (weight 0.45)

Two payments share an idempotency key, a wallet, a merchant and an amount inside the duplicate window. A retry created a second real debit.

Evidence fields: `idempotency_key`, `captured_minor`, `wallet_id`, `merchant_id`, `created_at`

### `R102_AUTH_CAPTURE_GAP`

**Captured amount does not match the authorisation** · family `integrity` · severity `medium` (weight 0.25)

The gap between authorisation and capture is beyond any ordinary tip or adjustment, so the two records disagree about how much the payer owes.

Evidence fields: `authorized_minor`, `captured_minor`, `presentment_currency`

### `R103_QUARANTINED_EVENTS`

**The feed sent events this transaction could not accept** · family `integrity` · severity `low` (weight 0.10)

One or more events were illegal for the state the transaction was in and were quarantined. The ledger is intact, but the upstream feed is not.

Evidence fields: `rejected_event_count`

### `R201_WALLET_VELOCITY`

**Payment velocity far above this wallet's own baseline** · family `velocity` · severity `high` (weight 0.45)

The wallet made more payments inside the velocity window than the policy allows, measured against its own history rather than a global average.

Evidence fields: `wallet_id`, `created_at`, `baseline_weekly_count`

### `R202_AMOUNT_ANOMALY`

**Amount far above this wallet's usual ticket** · family `velocity` · severity `high` (weight 0.45)

The payment is many times the wallet's typical value. Large is not suspicious; large *for this payer* is.

Evidence fields: `captured_minor`, `presentment_currency`, `wallet_id`

### `R301_GEO_MISMATCH`

**IP country differs from the wallet's registered country** · family `geo_device` · severity `low` (weight 0.10)

The payment came from outside the wallet's home jurisdiction. Most people who do this are on holiday. Severity is deliberately low: routing every foreign-IP payment to a human would bury the queue in tourists and teach analysts to click through. It carries weight only in combination with something else.

Evidence fields: `ip_country`, `wallet_country`

### `R302_DEVICE_HOPPING`

**One wallet paying from several devices in one hour** · family `geo_device` · severity `high` (weight 0.45)

Three or more distinct device fingerprints inside an hour is a pattern of credential sharing or automated use, not of one person paying.

Evidence fields: `device_id`, `wallet_id`, `created_at`

### `R303_IMPOSSIBLE_TRAVEL`

**Impossible travel between consecutive payments** · family `geo_device` · severity `critical` (weight 0.70)

The distance between two consecutive payments requires a speed no aircraft reaches. The two payments cannot both have been made by a person in transit.

Evidence fields: `ip_country`, `created_at`, `wallet_id`

### `R304_JURISDICTION_CONFLICT`

**Wallet, payer and IP jurisdictions all disagree on a low-KYC wallet** · family `geo_device` · severity `high` (weight 0.45)

Three different countries on a wallet that has only basic verification. Each alone is ordinary; together on a thin identity they are not.

Evidence fields: `wallet_country`, `payer_country`, `ip_country`, `kyc_level`

### `R401_FX_OUT_OF_TOLERANCE`

### `R501_MCC_TICKET_ANOMALY`

**Ticket size far outside the merchant's declared category** · family `merchant_profile` · severity `high` (weight 0.45)

### `R601_REFUND_CHAIN`

**Repeated refunds between the same payer and merchant** · family `dispute_abuse` · severity `medium` (weight 0.25)

Several refunds between one pair inside a short window. A refund is ordinary; a chain of them between the same two parties is a known laundering shape.

Evidence fields: `refunded_minor`, `wallet_id`, `merchant_id`, `created_at`

### `R602_DOUBLE_CREDIT`

**Refunded and charged back - the payer was credited twice** · family `dispute_abuse` · severity `critical` (weight 0.70)

The payment was refunded by the merchant and then reversed by the issuer. The merchant has paid for this transaction twice.

Evidence fields: `refunded_minor`, `charged_back_minor`, `captured_minor`

### `R701_SHARED_PAYOUT_ACCOUNT`

**Merchant shares a payout account with other merchants** · family `network_linkage` · severity `medium` (weight 0.25)

Several supposedly unrelated merchants settle to one beneficiary account. Shared devices are common; shared payout accounts are not. Severity is medium rather than high because this is a fact about the *merchant*, true of every payment it ever takes: a franchise group and a fraud ring look identical here, and only the rest of the case separates them.

Evidence fields: `payout_account_id`, `merchant_id`

### `R702_SHARED_FUNDING_ACCOUNT`

**Wallet shares a funding account with other wallets** · family `network_linkage` · severity `medium` (weight 0.25)

Evidence fields: `account_id`, `wallet_id`

### `R802_UNTRUSTED_INSTRUCTIONS`

**Free text contains instructions aimed at an automated reader** · family `completeness` · severity `high` (weight 0.45)

Merchant-supplied text is attacker-controlled. Text that addresses a model rather than a person is treated as an attack surface and quarantined before any AI reads it.

**Free text claims an approval that is not in the system** · family `completeness` · severity `high` (weight 0.45)

Evidence fields: `merchant_note`
